A gift — for you, and only you

For Radwa.

There are things that cannot be said out loud.
This is an attempt, anyway.

A note, before we begin

There was no moment I could point to,

only the realization that with you,
things that used to feel heavy
did not feel the same anymore,
I Love you.

What you are about to see
is not a gift made in a day.
It is made of every ordinary moment
that turned out to be anything but.
